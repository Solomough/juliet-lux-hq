# Juliet Lux HQ

Design and develop a modern, premium, luxury-inspired digital commerce website for Chill Fit by Juliet Lux, a Nigerian fashion and beauty brand. brand logo uploaded

This is not just an e-commerce website.

It is a Digital Business Headquarters that allows customers to effortlessly browse fashion collections, book makeup appointments, discover the brand story, and place orders through WhatsApp with minimal friction.

The design should feel luxurious, elegant, feminine, soft, trustworthy, modern, and premium.

The overall experience should rival premium lifestyle brands while remaining simple and intuitive for everyday Nigerian customers.

Everything must be mobile-first since most visitors will come from WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok.

FINAL GOAL

Create a premium digital commerce experience that immediately communicates elegance, trust, confidence, and professionalism. Visitors should feel as though they are interacting with a high-end lifestyle brand rather than a typical boutique website. Every interaction—from browsing products to booking makeup services and placing WhatsApp orders—should be seamless, intuitive, and visually delightful.

Important: Structure the project as a reusable, scalable commerce platform that can later serve as a template for other fashion boutiques, salons, beauty brands, and lifestyle businesses with minimal customization. This ensures maintainability, future feature expansion, and code reusability while delivering a polished, production-quality user experience.
